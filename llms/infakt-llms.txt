# inFakt

> inFakt is a Polish fintech platform that combines invoicing software, online accounting, and bookkeeper services for sole proprietors (JDG), LLCs (sp. z o.o.), foundations, and other business types operating in Poland. Used by over 460,000 companies, inFakt offers a web app, mobile apps (iOS/Android), KSeF integration (Poland's National e-Invoice System), and a REST API for third-party integrations. Headquartered in Kraków, Poland.

inFakt operates in the Polish market and all product interfaces, support, and most content are in Polish. The platform's core value proposition is simplifying accounting, invoicing, tax compliance, and KSeF e-invoicing for small and medium businesses in Poland. Key differentiator: combining an intuitive self-service app with access to dedicated personal accountants ("Księgowy z polecenia") vetted through a rigorous 3-stage recruitment process.

## Supported Invoice Types

inFakt supports: standard invoices (faktura), proforma, scheduled invoices, recurring invoices, advance invoices (zaliczkowa), corrective invoices (korygująca), settlement invoices (rozliczeniowa), margin invoices (marża), OSS invoices, internal invoices, and accounting notes (DW). Invoices can be issued in foreign currencies.

## Key Integrations

- KSeF (Krajowy System e-Faktur) – Poland's National e-Invoice System
- Bank account connectivity via AIS/PSD2 (licensed AISP by KNF), including Santander Bank integration
- E-commerce: WooCommerce, Shoper, Shoplo, Sky-Shop, BaseLinker, Apilo, TrisoSHOP, Sellasist, PartsManager
- Project management: Centrium, Mantreo
- Financial services: PragmaGO (installment payments), debt collection (windykacja)
- JPK (Jednolity Plik Kontrolny) generation and submission to tax authorities
- VAT-UE declarations
